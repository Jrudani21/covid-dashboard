Dashboard Views

Global KPIs

Total cases, active cases, recoveries, deaths, and case fatality rate at a glance.

180-Day Trend Analysis

🏆

Choropleth Map

Cases per million population worldwide on a yellow-to-red gradient.

🔍

Data Explorer

Searchable, filterable table of all countries with per-capita metrics.
